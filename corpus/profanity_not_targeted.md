# Non-Targeted Profanity and Casual Swearing

Readers frequently react emotionally to breaking news using informal or coarse language that is not directed at any individual or protected group. Our moderation system handles these posts using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Casual swearing used purely for emphasis does not automatically warrant removal on a general news forum.

If a comment contains up to two mild or moderate swear words directed only at a situation, object, or event rather than a person, publish the comment automatically. If a comment contains three or more explicit profanities, or uses graphic sexual or anatomical vulgarity even without a clear target, send to human review, and hide any post where profanity shifts into personal abuse.

For example, if a user writes that the city transit delays are a complete mess and a total joke using a mild swear word about the commute, publish it. If a post strings together repeated explicit profanities to vent about a policy, send to human review.
