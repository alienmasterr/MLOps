# Threats of Violence and Physical Harm

Credible or explicit threats of physical harm against individuals, public officials, journalists, or groups are strictly forbidden across all comment sections. Moderators resolve violence-related flags using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Preventing real-world harm takes absolute priority over open debate.

Any comment expressing a desire, plan, or encouragement to inflict physical injury, death, or property destruction on a specific target must be hidden immediately within five minutes of detection. If a comment uses violent metaphors or hyperbole without naming a target or expressing intent, send to human review to evaluate context.

For example, if a commenter states that a local council member deserves to be beaten up outside city hall tomorrow, hide the comment immediately. On the other hand, if a reader writes that a proposed tax bill will financially murder small businesses, publish the comment since it is clearly metaphorical.
