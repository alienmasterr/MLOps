# Identity-Based Attacks Against Protected Groups

Our news platform strictly prohibits dehumanizing, demeaning, or stereotyping people based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, nationality, or disability. Moderators classify identity-related comments into three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Protecting marginalized communities from abuse is essential to maintaining a safe public forum.

Any comment that equates a protected group with criminals, diseases, or subhuman traits, or calls for their exclusion from public life, must be hidden immediately with zero tolerance. If a comment makes an ambiguous generalization about a demographic group while discussing sociological statistics from the article, send to human review rather than hiding automatically.

For instance, if a commenter claims that immigrants from a specific religious background are inherently violent and should be barred from neighborhoods, hide the comment immediately. By contrast, if a reader discusses census data comparing employment rates across gender groups without derogatory framing, publish the comment.
