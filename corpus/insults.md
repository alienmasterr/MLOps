# Personal Insults Directed at Other Users

Comments on our news platform must focus on ideas rather than attacking fellow readers. When evaluating interpersonal hostility, moderators apply three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Vigorous disagreement over an article is acceptable and should be published, whereas direct name-calling or demeaning remarks aimed at another commenter degrade discussion quality and must be removed.

Any comment containing two or more derogatory personal labels directed at another user, or a single severe insult attacking intelligence or character, must be hidden immediately. If a comment expresses frustration with another user's argument without direct name-calling, send to human review.

For instance, if a commenter replies to another reader calling them an uneducated idiot who cannot read basic news, hide the comment. Conversely, if a commenter writes that another user's reasoning is completely flawed and poorly informed, publish the reply.
