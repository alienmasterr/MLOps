# Reclaimed Terms Used by Community Members

Certain words that historically functioned as derogatory labels have been reclaimed by members of the affected communities as expressions of solidarity or self-identification. Moderators process comments containing potentially reclaimed language using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Because intent and speaker context cannot be verified automatically, careful review is required.

Automated classifiers must never auto-publish a comment containing a known historically derogatory term, nor should they auto-hide it when there are no accompanying hostile verbs or second-person pronouns. Instead, route all such instances directly to human review within one hour, and hide the comment if the term is directed aggressively at another person.

For instance, if a commenter uses a reclaimed community label while sharing their personal experience attending a local pride march, send to human review so a moderator can confirm non-hostile self-reference and publish it. If another user hurls that same term at a commenter during an argument, hide it immediately.
