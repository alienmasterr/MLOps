# Spam, Commercial Promotion, and External Links

Unsolicited advertisements, repetitive promotional messages, and suspicious external links disrupt genuine news discussions and expose readers to scams. Moderators manage link-bearing and promotional posts using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Keeping threads free of commercial clutter preserves trust in the comment section.

Any comment containing affiliate links, cryptocurrency promotions, discount codes, or identical text posted more than twice across articles within one hour must be hidden automatically. If a comment includes a single external link to a reputable news, academic, or government domain relevant to the article, publish it; links to unfamiliar domains should go to human review.

For instance, if an account posts a link promising guaranteed daily crypto returns beneath a sports story, hide the comment immediately. If a reader links to a peer-reviewed climate study to support their point on an environmental article, publish it.
