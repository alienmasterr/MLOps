# Mentions of Self-Harm and Suicide

Discussions touching on suicide or self-injury require careful, compassionate handling to avoid encouraging harm while still allowing constructive awareness discussions. Moderators evaluate self-harm references using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Preventing instruction or encouragement of self-harm is our primary responsibility.

Any comment that encourages, instructs, or glorifies self-harm or suicide, or expresses immediate personal intent to self-harm, must be hidden immediately and flagged for crisis resource outreach. If a comment discusses mental health policy, survivor recovery, or prevention statistics from the article without graphic methods, publish it; ambiguous expressions of personal distress must be sent to human review within fifteen minutes.

For instance, if a commenter tells another user to end their life or describes methods of self-injury, hide the comment immediately. Conversely, if a reader shares how community counseling programs helped their recovery after reading a mental health report, publish the comment.
