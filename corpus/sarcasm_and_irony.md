# Sarcasm and Irony That Appear Toxic Out of Context

Sarcastic or ironic remarks frequently use harsh words to mean the exact opposite, causing automated classifiers to misread benign commentary as abusive. Moderators resolve suspected sarcasm using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Distinguishing playful irony from genuine hostility prevents unnecessary suppression of valid viewpoints.

When a comment contains apparent praise paired with an obvious negative indicator, or ironic exaggeration directed at an institutional failure rather than a person, send to human review if the toxicity score exceeds the medium threshold; otherwise publish it. Hide the comment only if the sarcastic phrasing conveys a genuine insult or threat toward an individual or protected group.

For example, if a commenter writes that waiting six hours in an emergency room is truly the pinnacle of modern healthcare efficiency, publish the comment as ironic policy criticism. If a user sarcastically congratulates another commenter on having the lowest intelligence in the thread, hide it.
