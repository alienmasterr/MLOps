# Criticism of Named Public Figures and Officials

Elected officials, corporate executives, celebrities, and other public figures naturally attract sharp scrutiny in news comment sections. Moderators handle comments naming public figures using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Public figures are subject to a higher tolerance for harsh criticism than private readers.

Critical remarks directed at a named public figure regarding their official actions, public statements, or professional competence should be published, even when phrased bluntly. However, if a comment includes death wishes, threats of physical harm, or hateful slurs targeting a public figure's protected identity, hide the comment immediately; send borderline personal attacks on a public figure's family members to human review.

For instance, if a commenter calls the mayor incompetent and dishonest over a delayed infrastructure project, publish the comment. If a user posts that the mayor should be assaulted at the next press conference, hide the comment immediately.
