# Satire, Parody, and Humorous Commentary

Readers often respond to absurd or lighthearted news stories with jokes, parody, or satirical exaggeration. Moderators classify humorous posts using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Over-filtering humor makes comment sections feel sterile, while under-filtering allows abuse disguised as jokes.

If a joke or satirical scenario clearly targets a public policy, bureaucratic absurdity, or fictional premise without demeaning a protected group or threatening real people, publish it. When a comment contains dark humor or borderline mockery where satirical intent is unclear to an automated classifier, send to human review; hide any joke that relies on racial, religious, or disability stereotypes.

For example, if a reader jokes beneath an article about pothole repairs that local drivers will soon need lunar rovers to commute to work, publish the comment. If someone posts a joke mocking people with physical disabilities, hide it.
