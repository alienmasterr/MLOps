# Doxxing and Exposure of Private Personal Information

Publishing another person's private, non-public identifying information puts individuals at severe risk of offline harassment, identity theft, or physical danger. Moderators treat potential doxxing reports using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Privacy protection applies equally to private citizens and public figures.

Any comment containing a home address, personal phone number, private email address, social security number, or workplace schedule of a private individual must be hidden immediately upon detection. If a comment shares a publicly listed official office contact number of a government representative, send to human review to verify that it is genuinely public before publishing.

For example, if a commenter posts the residential street address and personal cell number of a witness quoted in a local crime story, hide the comment immediately. If a reader shares the published switchboard number of a state senator's office, send to human review.
