# Harsh Criticism of Political Parties and Public Policies

Robust, strongly worded debate about legislation, government budgets, and political parties is central to civic discourse on a news platform. Moderators evaluate intense political commentary using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Strong ideological opposition is protected speech as long as it targets institutions rather than individuals with abuse.

Comments that sharply criticize a political party, government agency, or proposed law—even using words like corrupt, incompetent, or disastrous—must be published immediately provided there are zero threats of violence or attacks on protected demographic groups. Send to human review only if the comment ambiguously conflates a political party with an ethnic or religious group.

For example, if a reader writes that the ruling party's housing bill is an utter failure that will bankrupt working families, publish the comment immediately. If a commenter claims that supporters of a specific party deserve to be physically driven out of town, hide the comment.
