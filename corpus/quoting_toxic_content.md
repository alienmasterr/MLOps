# Quoting Toxic Language to Criticize or Refute It

Readers sometimes quote offensive remarks from an article or another commenter in order to condemn, fact-check, or analyze the statement. Moderators handle quoted toxicity using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Automated filters often flag these posts because they contain sensitive keywords, so contextual evaluation is critical.

If a comment encloses a short quoted phrase inside quotation marks and devotes at least seventy percent of the total text to clearly condemning or analyzing the statement, publish or send to human review rather than hiding automatically. However, if the quote repeats severe hate speech or threats verbatim without meaningful commentary, hide the comment.

For instance, if a reader quotes a politician's derogatory remark from the news story and writes a paragraph explaining why such rhetoric harms public trust, send to human review so a moderator can verify the critical intent and publish it. If a user simply pastes an abusive rant with a laughing emoji, hide it.
