# Repeated Harassment and Targeted Following

Persistent, unwanted attention directed at a single community member undermines healthy participation even when individual messages appear mild on their own. Moderators address sustained targeting using three outcomes: publish, send to human review, or hide. Patterns across threads matter just as much as isolated wording.

If an author posts three or more unreciprocated replies within a twenty-four-hour period targeting the same user with mocking, dismissive, or intrusive remarks, hide the third and subsequent comments and send the thread to human review. Single disagreements within a normal back-and-forth exchange should be published unless they cross into personal insults.

For instance, if User A follows User B across four separate news articles in one afternoon leaving mocking remarks about User B's previous posts, hide the repeated replies and send the account pattern to human review. If two users debate an energy article across two polite replies, publish both comments.
